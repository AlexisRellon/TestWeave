# TestWeave logo exploration

Audience: developers, QA teams, and maintainers using the CLI, MCP server, and local viewer. Product promise: keep manual QA scenarios in step with source changes.

Assumed character: precise, connected, dependable, quietly distinctive. Primary settings: README header, browser tab, package avatar, and local viewer. Exact spelling: TestWeave. Colour is deferred until a direction is chosen.

Avoid category defaults: standalone checkmarks, shields, test tubes, code brackets, AI sparkles, and gradient blobs.

Word map: weave → crossing, thread, continuity; testing → evidence, agreement, precision; drift → separation, alignment; contract → shared boundary, connection.

## Longlist

Scores are clarity / distinction / simplicity / relevance / small-size strength, each out of five.

1. Woven W: a W with a deliberate crossing break at its centre. 5/4/5/5/4. Selected.
2. Cross-thread T: a T crossbar passes through a separate upright. 5/4/5/4/5. Selected.
3. Paired paths: two open, angular links share one diagonal axis. 4/4/5/5/4. Selected.
4. Registration: two offset corner marks settle around a shared square. 3/3/5/4/5.
5. Living seam: two document edges share a zigzag seam. 3/4/3/5/3.
6. Shuttle: a single diamond carries an interrupted thread. 3/3/4/3/4.
7. Twin baseline: the two words share a custom extended baseline. 4/3/4/4/2.
8. Contract fold: a folded page hides a W in its counter. 3/3/3/4/3.
9. Matched steps: two stair shapes form one continuous track. 4/3/5/4/4.

## Construction

A: a filled W on a 256-unit canvas, with a diagonal transparent separation across the central join. B: a 32-unit upright, a 40-unit crossbar, and two 16-unit clear gaps. C: two open square links rotated 45 degrees, with transparent crossing gaps. No raster effects or masks.

Library research: testing-monitoring category summary and construction studies of Framer, Kotlin, Patreon, and Weave. These are references for craft, not source artwork.

Concept lockups use live Segoe UI text for exploration only; typography and outlines will be resolved after selection. Symbols are independent filled vector shapes.

The supplied bug records concern HTTP origins, hook runtime versions, and npm publishing; none applies to isolated logo exploration. No application code, hook, release, or QA behavior is changed.
