# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds, experiences, and abilities. Everyone participating in TestWeave issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces is expected to:

- Treat people respectfully and assume good intent while taking reported harm seriously.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about work.
- Respect privacy, consent, and personal boundaries.
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions and help keep discussions focused.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information without consent, and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

The maintainer, [@AlexisRellon](https://github.com/AlexisRellon), is responsible for enforcement. For a confidential report, use the repository's [private reporting channel](https://github.com/AlexisRellon/TestWeave/security/advisories/new) and clearly title it **Code of Conduct report**. This channel is also used for security reports; conduct reports will be handled privately. Include links and context, and do not post another person's private information publicly.

The maintainer may remove content, request a correction, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude a participant according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports are handled with discretion; information is shared only as needed to investigate and respond. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited. A participant may request reconsideration through the same private channel.
